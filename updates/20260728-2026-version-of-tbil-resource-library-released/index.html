2026 Version of TBIL Resource Library Released

The 2026 versions of our TBIL activity books (for Precalculus, Calculus, and Linear Algebra) are now available in the TBIL Resource Library. These feature updates, corrections, and additions contributed by members of the TBIL Community over the past year.

We are also excited to announce that we are publishing a printed version of the Linear Algebra for Team-Based Inquiry Learning activity book. The official launch date is August 15, with preorders available now through 619 Wreath Publishing. The HTML version of the books will always remain freely available on this website in the TBIL Resource Library.
